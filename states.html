Component state gallery

Each interactive component in the registration flow, shown in all seven specified states.

Hover, focus, and active are forced here with helper classes so they can be compared side by side. In the live prototype they come from :hover, :focus-visible, and :active. A state marked not applicable explains why and where the equivalent feedback lives.

Primary button

Used for Search classes, Continue, Review schedule, and Register. One per screen region.

Default

Plum #4C2A85, white text, 48px tall

Hover

Darker #3D2070 plus 2px bottom edge

Focus

3px ink outline, 3px offset, :focus-visible

Active

#2C1554, inset shadow, moves down 1px

Add at least one section to review.

Disabled

aria-disabled="true" stays focusable; reason linked by aria-describedby

Loading

Label changes in the same frame as the click; spinner; repeat clicks ignored

!Check the box to confirm you understand the charges.

Error

Error is placed on the field that caused it; focus moves there; aria-live="assertive"

Add section button

Full. Seats open up when students drop.
